### claim 138
- **原论断**：IFN-γ, TNF-α and IL-2 spike-specific CD8+ T cell responses were shown to contribute in protecting RhM against reinfection [<|cit|>].
- **证据**：D01:S01;D03:S02;D06:S01;D07:S01
- **理由**：改判：claim说"CD8+ T cells expressing IFN-gamma, TNF-alpha, and IL-2 contribute to protection"，D01直接列出这三种细胞因子的S-specific CD8+ T反应，D06/D07支持CD8+ T对re-challenge保护有贡献；claim是说表达这三种因子的CD8+ T细胞整体贡献保护，不是要求每种因子独立贡献；原判决误读为三种因子各自独立贡献。

- **证据**：D03:S01;D04:S01;D04:S02

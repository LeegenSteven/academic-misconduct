SNR values were then normalized per channel to the mean per-channel summed SNR. PSMs having a coefficient of variation greater than 0.5 across control channels, or having a mean SNR of less than two across all channels were removed. Protein competition ratios were then calculated by grouping PSMs by protein, calculating the mean control condition SNR across replicate channels, and dividing that figure by the summed SNR for every other channel. Ratios were capped between −32 and 32 and converted to percent competition values. Percent competition values for serine hydrolases were reported only if the protein had at least three quantified peptides, and at least one unique quantified peptide.

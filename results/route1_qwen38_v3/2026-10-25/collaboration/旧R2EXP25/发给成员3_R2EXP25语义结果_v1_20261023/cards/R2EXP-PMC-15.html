原始施引段落

First, we analysed the genetic correlation between CFM and 33 diseases in an Asian population. We subsequently identified the polymorphic loci and genes involved in CFM and diseases genetically related to CFM via PLACO. In addition, we performed GO and KEGG enrichment analyses on these genes12,13. Finally, we used Two-sample Mendelian randomized analysis to analyse the causal relationship between CFM and tumours, respiratory disease, heart disease and kidney disease (Fig. 1).

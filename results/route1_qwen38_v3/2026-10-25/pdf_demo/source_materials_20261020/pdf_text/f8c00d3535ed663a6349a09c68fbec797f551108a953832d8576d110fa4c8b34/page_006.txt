Fourth, we acknowledge that our design only allowed establishing rela-

risk of death in our survival analyses.
Future studies should test whether our findings are generalizable 
to more diverse populations in terms of ethnicity, socioeconomic 
status and medical comorbidities. Furthermore, studies with longer 
follow-ups and larger samples of A+TNEO-T
+ and A+TMTL
+ cognitively unim-
paired individuals will help refine the current findings. This may, in 
turn, aid in enriching clinical trials for fast progressors and developing 
algorithms for a personalized prognosis that reliably estimates the risk 
for future cognitive decline at an individual level. Of particular interest 
will be the performance of head-to-head studies between tau PET and 
high performing plasma p-tau assays44–47 to investigate the potential 
added value and cost-effectiveness of tau PET as a prognostic tool.

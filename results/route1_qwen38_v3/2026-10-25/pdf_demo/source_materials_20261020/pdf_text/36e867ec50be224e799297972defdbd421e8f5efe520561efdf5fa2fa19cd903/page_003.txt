Bubble detection is optional in AfterQC and is not
enabled by default. According to our study, Illumina
NextSeq sequencers are more likely to raise bubbles, so we
suggest enabling this option for NextSeq sequencer out-
puts and disabling it for HiSeq sequencer outputs. Figure 3
shows a part of debubble’s output, from which we can also
find that NextSeq sequencers produce much more polyX
reads.

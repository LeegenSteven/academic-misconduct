Filtering
After trimming is done, AfterQC will apply a series of
filters on the reads. AfterQC implements quality filters
and polyX filters. Quality filters are trivial, which just
count the number of low quality bases or N, calculate the
mean quality of each read, and then determine whether to
keep or discard this read. AfterQC implements an error-
tolerantly method to detect polyX (X is one of A/T/C/G).
Two arguments (P and L) are used to configure the polyX
detection algorithm, P (default is 35) means how long the
polyX sequence should have, while L (default is 2) refers
to how many non-X bases can be tolerated in each polyX

Text Box 2 AT(N)(C) measures have different roles for definition and staging
Definition
A: Aβ biomarkers determine whether or not an individual is in the Alzheimer’s 
continuum.
T: Pathologic tau biomarkers determine if someone who is in the Alzheimer’s 
continuum has Alzheimer’s disease.
Staging severity
(N): Neurodegenerative/neuronal injury biomarkers
(C): Cognitive symptoms
A and T indicate specific neuropathologic changes that define Alzheimer’s disease, 
whereas (N) and (C) are not specific to Alzheimer’s disease and are therefore placed in 
parentheses.

Fig. 3. 
Alzheimer’s and concomitant suspected non-Alzheimer’s pathologic change with dementia. 
A 91-year-old male with severe amnestic dementia. Participant in the Mayo Alzheimer’s 
Disease Research Center. Abnormal amyloid PET with Pittsburgh compound B (top row), 
normal tau PET (flortaucipir, middle row), and severe medial temporal atrophy on MRI 
(bottom row). The biomarker profile (A +T−(N)+) suggests the patient has Alzheimer’s 
pathologic change (A+T−) plus an additional degenerative condition [(N)+], likely 
hippocampal sclerosis.

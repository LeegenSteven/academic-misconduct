NOTE. Binarizing the three AT(N) biomarker types leads to eight different biomarker “profiles”. Every individual can be placed into one of the 
three general biomarker “categories” based on biomarker profiles: those with normal AD biomarkers (no color), those with non-AD pathologic 
change (dark grey), and those who are in the Alzheimer’s continuum (light grey). The term “Alzheimer’s continuum” is an umbrella term that 
denotes either Alzheimer’s pathologic change or AD.
NOTE. If an individual has an abnormal amyloid biomarker study, but a biomarker for tau is not available, then the individual is placed into the 
“Alzheimer’s continuum”. A missing biomarker group can be labeled with an asterisk (*). For example, A+(N)+ without a T biomarker would be A
+T*(N)+.

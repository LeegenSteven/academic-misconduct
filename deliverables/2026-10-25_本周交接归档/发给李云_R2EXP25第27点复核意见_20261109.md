施引 PMC11579624 正文（正文第 41 个 `<p>`，0-based ordinal 40，与记录一致）：

LLMs excel at producing coherent and contextually relevant text, yet they lack true comprehension or reasoning abilities. In the realm of oncology chatbots, this limitation can manifest in responses based solely on patterns from their training data, without a deep grasp of medical principles [93].

- “yet they lack true comprehension or reasoning abilities” 所在句无引用标注，属同段延伸表述，不能计为 [92] 支撑；也不能把 [93] 的支撑范围提前到这一句；

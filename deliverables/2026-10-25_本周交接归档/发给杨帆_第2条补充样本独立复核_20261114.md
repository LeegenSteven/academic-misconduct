施引完整段落：

> Emerging research implicates stress-induced gut dysbiosis in stoma-related complications. Animal models demonstrate that chronic stress alters gut microbiota composition, reducing microbial diversity and impairing mucosal immunity—a finding with direct relevance to ileostomy patients [9]. For these patients, the diverted digestive system and absence of the colon create a unique vulnerability to dysbiosis [10]. Stress-induced changes in gut microbiota can exacerbate complications such as small intestinal bacterial overgrowth (SIBO), leakage, and skin irritation around the stoma [3]. Furthermore, impaired mucosal immunity increases the risk of infections and delays wound healing, underscoring the bidirectional relationship between mental health and physical recovery in ileostomy patients [9, 10].

## 二、第一处 CR10-1

原句：

> For these patients, the diverted digestive system and absence of the colon create a unique vulnerability to dysbiosis [10].

该句把“消化道改道＋结肠缺失”作为“对菌群失调独特易感性”的原因，属特定解剖学命题。

被引核查：全文 ileostom 0、colectom 0、divert 0；stoma 唯一命中为 stomach；colon 23 处均为肠道生理或菌群语境，无“结肠缺失”。被引未涉及造口人群，也未讨论解剖改道与菌群失调易感性的关系。

判断：**NOT_ACCURATE（限定范围）**。被引可支持的仅为菌群组成受应激、饮食影响及菌群—肠脑轴互作的一般背景，与本句主题相关但不足以承担该特定命题；不判 IRRELEVANT，不保留待核（缺口属原文属性）。

## 三、第二处 CR10-2

原句：

> Furthermore, impaired mucosal immunity increases the risk of infections and delays wound healing, underscoring the bidirectional relationship between mental health and physical recovery in ileostomy patients [9, 10].

| 分句 | 被引核查 | 是否支持 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 黏膜免疫受损增加感染风险并延迟伤口愈合 | wound 0、healing 0、recovery 0、mucosal immunity 0；infection 相关 3 段均不构成该链条 | 不支持 |
| 精神健康与造口患者身体康复的双向关系 | 有肠—脑双向通讯论述（p0015、开篇 vice-versa）；无 ileostomy、无 recovery，未涉及造口人群与康复结局 | 一般背景相容，具体人群与结局不支持 |

被引可用作一般背景的原文：

> The complex and multifaceted system of gut-brain communication not only ensures proper maintenance and coordination of gastrointestinal functions to support behaviour and physiological processes, but also permits feedback from the gut to exert profound effects on mood, motivated behaviour, and higher cognitive functions. The linkage ... is afforded through afferent and efferent neural projection pathways, bi-directional neuroendocrine signaling, immune activation and signaling from gut to brain ...

> The immune system plays an important intermediary role in the dynamic equilibrium that exists between the brain and the gut ... the gut itself is an important immune organ that provides a vital defensive barrier between externally-derived pathogens and the internal biological environment.

判断：**NOT_ACCURATE（限定范围，仅评[10]）**。两个具体命题均超出被引范围；肠脑双向通讯与菌群—免疫互作的一般背景相容，不等于支持本句。
